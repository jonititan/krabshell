Weather

The wind the drones fly in, one series per A5 weather cell. A drone takes the wind of its own cell's ancestor at the weather resolution, at its height: it crabs into a crosswind, a headwind slows it, and its battery drains with the square of its airspeed. Drones stay on the ground when the wind or gusts at takeoff height are over the launch limits.
