Geofences

Keep-out zones, from the surface up or within an altitude band (metres above ground level; drones may fly beneath a fence whose floor is raised, or over one with a ceiling). The scenario starts with its own (world/geofences.json); draw more here, close any of them, and ground stations can upload their own as a fence plan. The drones the simulation flies are rerouted round each one by MAVLink; a drone found inside one in the air is a breach.
