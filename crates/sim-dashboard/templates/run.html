What the numbers mean

Sim rate
Ticks per second the driver is actually committing, measured over the last publish window.
Publishing
Snapshots per second offered to readers. Capped by run.publish_hz, whatever the tick rate.
Dropped views
Snapshots replaced before anything read them. A large number at maximum speed is expected, not a fault.
Intent queue
Admitted intents waiting for a tick. It never blocks the loop: a full queue rejects new intents instead.
